# Roadmap, PRD & Prototype (Module 4)

## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Frontline Fleet Coordinator responsible for managing daily deliveries, assigning routes, monitoring drivers, and resolving operational issues.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** Reduce the average time required to complete daily dispatch workflows.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** Complex workflows and unreliable route optimization force coordinators to perform manual corrections and rely on spreadsheets, external maps, and driver calls.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Maintain or improve compliance accuracy and on-time delivery rates while reducing workflow completion time.

## Scan the backlog & set a human baseline
- **My instinctive “quick wins” before touching the AI (2 to 3 feature IDs + why):** B1 – One-Click Compliance Checklist: Reduces the 14.6-minute compliance bottleneck.

B3 – Shift Handoff Wizard: Simplifies shift transitions and potentially saves 6.8 minutes per day.

B5 – Step Progress Indicator: Reduces workflow confusion and helps coordinators complete tasks more efficiently with relatively low implementation effort.

## Audit, override & decide
- **Where did you override the AI? (feature + old vs. new score + why):** I retained the original Value and Effort scores but changed the decisions for B6 (Driver Alert Notifications) and B7 (Contextual AI ETA Display) from Later to Cut. Neither feature directly addresses the coordinator's core workflow friction, and excluding them protects the limited four-week pilot scope.
- **Did the AI over-value a Sales/Eng request your M2 interviews don’t support?:** No. The AI correctly assigned B8 (Fleet Analytics Manager View) a low Value score of 1 and a high Effort score of 4. This Sales-driven feature serves management reporting needs rather than addressing the frontline coordinator's core pain points. I agreed with the recommendation to cut it.
- **Did it underweight something your M3 cohort/funnel data strongly supports?:** No. The AI correctly prioritized B1 (One-Click Compliance Checklist) with a Value score of 5 and B5 (Step Progress Indicator) with a Value score of 4. The 14.6-minute compliance bottleneck and the M3 funnel evidence support including both features in the pilot.
