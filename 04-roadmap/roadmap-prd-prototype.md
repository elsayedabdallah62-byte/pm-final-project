# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · RouteLogic Velocity

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer + 1 CS lead

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Frontline Fleet Coordinator
- **Primary metric:** Reduce average daily dispatch workflow completion time by 30%.
- **Moment of misery:** Complex workflows and unreliable route optimization force coordinators to perform manual corrections and rely on external tools.
- **Guardrail:** Maintain or improve the compliance checklist accuracy rate compared to the baseline.

## Roadmap
### NOW, Pilot (4 weeks, 3 accounts)
- **B1 One-Click Compliance Checklist**, Directly reduces the 14.6-minute compliance bottleneck while preserving checklist accuracy.
- **B3 Shift Handoff Wizard**, Saves approximately 6.8 minutes per day and simplifies shift handoffs.
- **B5 Step Progress Indicator**, Reduces workflow confusion and unnecessary navigation with minimal engineering effort.

### NEXT, GA Release (weeks 5-8)
- **B2 Smart Daily Report Auto-Fill**, Reduces manual reporting time but requires AI integration and accuracy validation.
- **B4 Mobile-First Coordinator Dashboard**, Simplifies core coordinator workflows but requires significant design and engineering effort.

### LATER, backlog
- **B10 In-App Coordinator Training**, Supports onboarding but does not eliminate the underlying workflow complexity.

### ✂ Cut List
- **B6 Driver Alert Notifications**, Improves driver communication but has a limited direct impact on workflow completion time.
- **B7 Contextual AI ETA Display**, Improves ETA visibility but does not directly address the coordinator's core workflow friction.
- **B8 Fleet Analytics Manager View**, Serves management reporting needs rather than the frontline coordinator's primary pain point.
- **B9 Compliance Audit Trail Export**, Supports regulatory audits but does not directly reduce daily dispatch workflow time.
