# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A frontline fleet coordinator responsible for managing daily delivery operations, monitoring drivers, and resolving real-time logistics issues.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Coordinate deliveries efficiently, monitor fleet activities, and resolve operational issues quickly with minimal manual effort.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** RouteLogic's complex interface and excessive features slow down critical daily tasks, forcing coordinators to rely on external tools and manual workarounds.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Google Maps for route planning, Excel or Google Sheets for tracking deliveries, and WhatsApp or other messaging apps for communicating with drivers and coordinating urgent changes.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Export delivery information from RouteLogic into spreadsheets.
Use Google Maps to verify routes and identify road closures.
Contact drivers through messaging apps to coordinate changes.
Manually update delivery statuses and track progress in spreadsheets.
Re-enter updated information into RouteLogic for reporting.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The most frustrating moment occurs when an urgent delivery issue requires the coordinator to switch between multiple tools, manually verify information, contact drivers, and update records, causing delays and duplicated work.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** UXR-05 (Sam, Driver) reports that RouteLogic recommends closed roads, ignores local access constraints, and cannot save route overrides. Sam also screenshots routes because the app is unusable offline. This demonstrates how frontline users rely on manual workarounds when RouteLogic fails to support daily operations.
