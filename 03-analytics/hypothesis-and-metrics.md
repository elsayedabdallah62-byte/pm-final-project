## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** Frontline coordinators struggle with RouteLogic's complex interface and inefficient daily workflows. They rely on spreadsheets, external maps, and manual route corrections because the platform does not adequately support their operational needs.
- **Quantitative (M3):** Only 31% of coordinators complete Shift Handoff, while Compliance Checks take 14.6 minutes versus a 3-minute benchmark. Coordinator NPS declined from +18 to -12, and daily time lost increased from 9 to 31 minutes.

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** Frontline Fleet Coordinator responsible for managing daily delivery operations, monitoring drivers, assigning routes, and resolving real-time logistics issues.
- **Goal:** Coordinate daily deliveries efficiently, assign routes, monitor fleet activities, and resolve operational issues quickly with minimal manual effort.
- **Friction:** Complex and time-consuming workflows, excessive manual steps, and inefficient compliance and shift handoff processes prevent coordinators from completing daily operations efficiently.
- **Problem you are solving:** RouteLogic's fragmented and time-consuming daily workflows prevent coordinators from completing critical dispatch, compliance, and shift handoff tasks efficiently.

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** Increase daily workflow completion, reduce manual workarounds and operational delays, improve coordinator satisfaction, and lower customer churn risk.
- **Primary success metric:** Increase Coordinator Shift Handoff Completion Rate from 31% to at least 50% during the pilot, representing a 19-percentage-point improvement (+61% relative increase).
- **Guardrail metric:** Maintain Coordinator Core Dispatch CSAT at or above the current baseline of 4.1/5 while simplifying workflows.
- **Decision window:** Run a 6-week pilot with frontline coordinators. Scale if Shift Handoff Completion reaches at least 50% while Core Dispatch CSAT remains at or above 4.1/5. Otherwise, review the results and refine the solution before expanding.
